Speaking

A speaker who has lived
the topics she talks about.

The talks sit at the intersection of technology, leadership, and the deliberate design of a career and life. Grounded in 19 years of practice — not theory.

Keynote  ·  Panel  ·  Workshop  ·  Podcast

Every session draws on experience as a senior leader at EY, a founder, a mother, and a woman who has navigated all of these identities at the same time. The talks don't offer theory. They offer what it actually looks like — the decisions, the tradeoffs, the design principles that held.

Topics

  • The motherhood advantage: how having a child sharpens the skills that matter most in leadership
  • Designing your career architecture: building a professional life that holds the personal one without deciding between them
  • AI and the human pace: why the real transformation challenge isn't technical, it's organizational
  • The leaky pipeline: what organizations must do to retain mothers in tech and leadership
  • Redesign, not balance: a new framework for ambitious women navigating multiple identities
